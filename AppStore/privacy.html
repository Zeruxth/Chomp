Chomp Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

The short version

Chomp doesn't collect anything. Your files never leave your Mac.

Your files

Chomp converts, resizes and compresses files entirely on your Mac, using Apple's built-in frameworks. Files are never uploaded, sent or shared with anyone, including us. Chomp only opens files you drop on it or pick yourself, and saves new files to Downloads/Chomped or to a folder you choose.

Data we collect

None. Chomp has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no network connections.

What stays on your Mac

  • Your settings, like your chest skin, quality and where to save files.
  • A count of how many files you've fed Chomp, used to unlock the Royal skin.
  • Permission for folders you've allowed Chomp to save into.

These are stored by macOS for the app and are removed when you delete Chomp.

AirDrop and sharing

If you choose AirDrop, macOS sends the file to the device you pick. Chomp itself sees nothing about that transfer.
