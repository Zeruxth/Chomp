Chomp

A file converter for Mac that happens to be a treasure chest.

Drag a file, tap Shift, and pick what you want it to become: a JPG, a PDF, or a video small enough for Discord. Chomp makes it and puts it next to the original.

Free · 1.3 MB · macOS 13 or newer

It'll also pretend to eat files you drop on this page.

Level 1

How it works

Step 1

Start dragging a file.

From Finder, your Desktop, anywhere.

⇧
Step 2

Tap Shift.

A wheel pops up. (⌘ gets you tools like compress, ⌃ gets you AirDrop and zip.)

Step 3

Drop it on what you want.

The new file shows up next to the old one.

Level 2

What it can do

Photos

Turn them into JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC or PDF. Make them smaller, resize for Instagram, cut out the background.

Videos

MP4, MOV, GIF. Shrink one under 10 MB, mute it, or grab a still.

Audio

MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC. Even out the volume, trim the silence.

PDFs

Merge them, split them, turn pages into images or text.

Anything else

AirDrop it, zip it, copy its path.

Snacks

Every file comes with a free snack for Chomp. There are ten. The brick is the crunchiest.

Video and audio need ffmpeg for now.

Level 3

Skins

Comes in six colours. One of them you have to earn by feeding it 100 files.

Level 4

Good to know

Works offline. Your files never leave your Mac.
Your original file stays untouched.
Made a mistake? There's an Undo button.

Opening it the first time

Chomp is free, so Apple grumbles the first time you open it. Click Done, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway.
