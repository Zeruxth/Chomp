What's new

Every release, newest first. Chomp tells you itself when a new one is out.

0.6

Latest31 October 2026

New tricks

  • Trim and crop videos. Pick the part to keep and a shape (square, 9:16, 16:9) in a little editor.
  • Words in pictures. The Text slice reads a screenshot or photo and copies the words for you.
  • Size limit. Choose 10, 25 or 50 MB and the fit slice aims under it. Compress also keeps images under it.
  • Watch a folder. New files that land in it get chewed automatically, like turning phone photos into JPGs.
  • Shrink copied pictures. Copy a big screenshot, paste a small JPG. Off until you switch it on.
  • Choose your slices. Hide the options you never use in More settings.
  • Seven languages. English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese and Chinese, following your computer's language.

Mac

  • Video and audio work out of the box. ffmpeg now comes inside the app, so there's nothing to install.
  • Chomp it in Finder's right-click menu, under Quick Actions.

Both

  • A once-a-day check for new versions, shown at the top of the panel. You can turn it off.
  • Fitting a small video under the limit no longer makes it bigger.

0.5

30 October 2026

Windows catches up

  • Drag anywhere, tap a key. Shift, Ctrl or Alt mid-drag opens the wheel under your cursor.
  • Cut out backgrounds, on your PC.
  • Documents between PDF, DOCX, RTF, TXT, HTML, ODT and Markdown, and Combine into one PDF.
  • Share opens the Windows share sheet. HEIC works with Microsoft's free codecs.
  • A pixel toast at the bottom of the screen, with Show and Undo.
